## 1. Dashboard

Explain the mixed six-instrument state, then point to governed Event/Knowledge Time, state counts, drivers, counter evidence, and the visibly separate live Binance overlay. Mention that live price does not recompute Evidence.

## 2. Markets

Open BTCUSDT from the six-instrument overview. Compare governed price, OI, Funding, aggressive flow, state, Evidence strength, Coverage, and frozen-corpus freshness across all six instruments.

## 3. Scanner

## 4. Trade

Show that the candidate persists in the URL and through reload. Walk through supporting facts, counter evidence, risk factors, observable invalidation, and the explicit `CONTEXT_ONLY_NO_ACTION` boundary.

## 5. Replay

Open the related 2026-07-11 UTC window. Move the synchronized cursor; show price, OI, the discrete provider-native Funding event, AggTrades aggregate, Evidence marker, and classified unavailable Liquidation/Order Book/news lanes.

## 6. Research

Open Evidence Packet `epkt_ba3fdfbdd77e34fea10b70a29d9f67c52ed6409725d1e0258fec3f711a82bd81`. Distinguish verified facts from interpretation, give counter evidence equal attention, explain LOW as Evidence strength rather than probability, then open lineage/reproducibility details.

## Honest Limitations

Complete individual liquidation-event history is source-blocked. Historical Order Book reconstruction is unavailable in the request path. The corpus is frozen and bounded. D2 source publication remains PENDING while selected Consumer Projections are visible.

If the live Binance overlay is unavailable, continue unchanged using the governed reference value and its as-of timestamp. The demo does not depend on the live overlay.
