# Trade Information Hierarchy

**Status:** Canonical screen hierarchy  
**Screen:** Trade  
**Purpose:** Execution support  

## Purpose

Trade answers:

```text
How should I evaluate this selected candidate?
```

Trade supports planning. It does not execute trades, sell signals, or replace
human judgment.

## Hierarchy

| Level | Information | Why it exists |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| Level 1 | Trade Thesis | States the candidate-specific thesis and context. |
| Level 2 | Evidence | Shows source-backed support and limitations. |
| Level 3 | Risk | Makes invalidation, uncertainty, and exposure visible. |
| Level 4 | Scenario Analysis | Helps users think through possible outcomes without predicting certainty. |
| Level 5 | Execution Notes | Organizes user-owned planning details. |
| Level 6 | Historical Analog | Provides source-backed comparison only when available. |

## Information Rules

- Trade is execution support, not broker execution.
- User retains decision authority.
- Evidence appears before scenarios or notes.
- Risk and invalidation must be visible.
- Historical analog remains unavailable unless source-backed.

## Primary Handoffs

| User need | Handoff |
| --- | --- |
| Review candidate origin | Scanner |
| Validate historical context | Replay |
| Research thesis | Research |
| Monitor live confirmation | Markets |

## Validation

Trade aligns with:

- Human Decision Authority;
- Evidence First;
- Explain, Don't Predict;
- no broker execution;
- selected candidate stability.
