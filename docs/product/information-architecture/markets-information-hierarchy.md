# Markets Information Hierarchy

**Status:** Canonical screen hierarchy  
**Screen:** Markets  
**Purpose:** Market monitoring  

## Purpose

Markets answers:

```text
Which markets deserve attention, and does live structure confirm it?
```

Markets is real-time first. It owns monitoring, verification, and market
structure, not deep historical research.

## Hierarchy

| Level | Information | Why it exists |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| Level 1 | Overview | Provides the live market monitoring state. |
| Level 2 | Sectors | Groups markets into durable context and relative strength/weakness. |
| Level 3 | Flows | Shows capital, volume, or movement context when source-backed. |
| Level 4 | Derivatives | Provides funding, OI, liquidation, and leverage context. |
| Level 5 | Macro | Adds source-backed macro context only when available. |
| Level 6 | Prediction Markets | Adds probability evidence without turning it into prediction. |
| Level 7 | Evidence | Shows source, freshness, availability, and warnings. |

## Information Rules

- Markets is real-time first.
- Historical-heavy processing belongs in Replay or Research.
- Derivatives evidence must preserve source/freshness state.
- Prediction market information is evidence, not forecast.
- Macro is unavailable unless source-backed.

## Primary Handoffs

| User need | Handoff |
| --- | --- |
| Discover opportunity | Scanner |
| Validate historical movement | Replay |
| Research context | Research |
| Plan trade candidate | Trade |

## Validation

Markets aligns with:

- Visual First;
- derivatives evidence clusters;
- heatmap scanning with accessibility;
- live monitoring ownership;
- no prediction overclaim.
