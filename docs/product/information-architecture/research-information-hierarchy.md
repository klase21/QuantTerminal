# Research Information Hierarchy

**Status:** Canonical screen hierarchy  
**Screen:** Research  
**Purpose:** Deep understanding  

## Purpose

Research answers:

```text
Why should I believe this thesis, and what contradicts it?
```

Research is the deepest product workspace before raw repository review. It
organizes evidence, reasoning, counter-evidence, sources, and historical
context without replacing user judgment.

## Hierarchy

| Level | Information | Why it exists |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| Level 1 | Headline | Frames the thesis or investigation question. |
| Level 2 | Executive Summary | Provides concise orientation for the research path. |
| Level 3 | Evidence | Shows supporting source-backed observations. |
| Level 4 | Reasoning | Connects evidence only when approved and clearly bounded. |
| Level 5 | Charts | Visualizes trends, flows, comparisons, and context. |
| Level 6 | Counter Evidence | Prevents one-sided interpretation and supports trust. |
| Level 7 | Sources | Shows source identity, freshness, methodology, and limitations. |
| Level 8 | Repository | Provides raw records and audit trail. |

## Information Rules

- Evidence appears before reasoning.
- Counter-evidence must be first-class when available.
- Missing evidence is not neutrality.
- Research may be dense, but summary must remain readable.
- Research should link back to Dashboard, Replay, Trade, or raw Repository
  context.

## Primary Handoffs

| User need | Handoff |
| --- | --- |
| Inspect historical window | Replay |
| Validate source records | Repository |
| Turn thesis into candidate plan | Trade |
| Return to current market state | Dashboard or Markets |

## Validation

Research aligns with:

- Evidence First;
- Explain, Don't Predict;
- Human Decision Authority;
- Research chart narrative;
- source transparency;
- counter-evidence discipline.
