does not rank companies as businesses. It identifies product patterns relevant

## Comparative Takeaways

1. Bloomberg, Koyfin, and Kaiko are strongest references for professional trust.
2. TradingView is the strongest reference for direct visual interaction.
3. CoinGlass and CoinAnk are the strongest references for derivatives density.
4. Arkham and Nansen are the strongest references for entity and wallet intelligence.
5. Glassnode, CryptoQuant, and Token Terminal are strongest for metric-based research.
6. DefiLlama is strongest for crypto category organization.
7. Polymarket is strongest for event/probability card framing.
8. Dune is strongest for query-backed transparency and community analytics.

## QuantTerminal Direction

QuantTerminal should combine:

- Bloomberg/Koyfin professional workflow;
- TradingView visual interaction;
- CoinGlass derivatives evidence;
- Arkham/Nansen investigation patterns;
- Glassnode/CryptoQuant metric context;
- DefiLlama category clarity;
- Polymarket event-card readability;
- Dune source/query transparency;
- Kaiko data-quality posture.

QuantTerminal should not copy:

- black-box confidence;
- engagement-first social feeds;
- unbounded dashboard density;
- unclear source provenance;
- action pressure without evidence;
- community analytics without review boundaries.
