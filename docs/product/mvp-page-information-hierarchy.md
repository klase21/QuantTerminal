# MVP Page Information Hierarchy

## Dashboard

Market state, state counts, primary drivers, primary risks, six-instrument overview, then Coverage/lineage disclosure. The first viewport explains that mixed categories are not averaged into a directional score.

## Markets

Six comparable instrument surfaces use the same order: governed price, bounded change, OI, Funding, aggressive flow, market state, Evidence strength, Coverage, freshness, and drill-downs.

## Scanner

Investigation-priority disclaimer precedes the ranked queue. Every row shows state, reason codes, Evidence strength, counter-evidence strength, Coverage comparability, freshness, and Evidence/Trade destinations.

## Trade

Explicit no-execution context, selected instrument/state, supporting facts, counter evidence, risks, observable invalidation, Evidence strength, Coverage/limitations, then Research and Replay links.

## Replay

Bounded window and synchronized cursor, price and OI lanes, discrete provider-native Funding markers, AggTrades aggregate, Evidence markers, then quieter classified unavailable enrichment. Raw AggTrades and request-time Order Book reconstruction remain absent.

## Research

Governed conclusion and Evidence-strength semantics, verified facts beside structured interpretation, support beside counter evidence, Confidence components, Coverage, then collapsed lineage and reproducibility detail.
