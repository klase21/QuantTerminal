# MVP Card Productization

## Default Hierarchy

Cards present: governed state, primary value, important changes, one-line
reason, Evidence strength and Coverage, drill-down actions, then expandable
technical evidence. State and Evidence strength are the default badges.

Repeated corpus labels, projection IDs, packet IDs, checksums, generator
versions, source digests, and technical reason codes do not dominate primary
cards. Material source limitations remain visible.

## Page Decisions

- Dashboard leads with market state, drivers, risks, investigation universe,
  and concise supplemental context.
- Markets uses one stable six-card order and separates governed reference from
  live overlay.
- Scanner is an investigation queue, never a signal board.
- Trade is a non-execution context workspace; duplicate risk/counter content is
  consolidated and invalidation is plain language.
- Research separates Fact from Interpretation and places lineage, versions,
  checksums, and low-level measurements under Technical Evidence.
- Replay receives bounded formatting only; its deeper timeline productization
  is deferred to MVP-6B.

## Figma Boundary

The connected Figma frames are the visual default. Adaptations preserve real
data, classified missing states, accessibility, responsive layout, performance,
live/governed separation, and protected runtime behavior.
