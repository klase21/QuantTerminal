# Competitive Journey Audit

**Status:** Canonical F5.5 comparative audit  
**Owner:** Product Intelligence  
**Basis:** QuantTerminal Competitive Intelligence Repository and approved V2 product contracts

## Method

This audit compares reusable journey qualities, not dataset breadth, brand reach, or unverified commercial capabilities. Ratings describe the documented QuantTerminal journey relative to the patterns identified in the canonical competitor studies.

## Journey Comparison

| Product | Workflow | Navigation | Context continuity | Evidence | Professional usability | QuantTerminal position |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bloomberg | Deep, command/workspace-oriented | Extremely powerful but learned | Strong workspace persistence | Institutional provenance varies by function | Exceptional for expert density | QuantTerminal is clearer and more guided; Bloomberg remains the benchmark for keyboard, workspace, and breadth. |
| TradingView | Chart-centered discovery to analysis | Excellent symbol and chart routing | Strong symbol/timeframe continuity | Mostly chart/indicator-led | Exceptional chart productivity | QuantTerminal is stronger in evidence/counter-evidence; TradingView remains stronger in chart tooling and customization. |

| Arkham | Entity/flow investigation | Graph-led | Excellent entity context | Strong traceability | Excellent investigation workflow | QuantTerminal matches evidence discipline at the product layer; Arkham remains stronger in entity graphs. |

## QuantTerminal Differentiation

QuantTerminal's defensible journey is:

```text
Orientation -> Live Verification -> Prioritization -> Historical Reconstruction
  -> Evidence Investigation -> User Decision
```

Its differentiation is not maximum data density. It is the continuous preservation of evidence, contradiction, availability, and ownership across specialized workspaces.

## Better / Equal / Needs Improvement

| Dimension | Assessment | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Evidence-before-reasoning | Better | Mandatory source, freshness, availability, contradiction, and Repository paths are cross-product rules. |
| Investigation ownership | Better | Each screen owns one durable question and hands off the next. |
| Navigation clarity | Equal | Stable primary navigation and guided drilldowns are strong; implementation still needs universal return trails. |
| Context preservation | Equal at design level | Canonical envelope is strong; competitor workspaces have more mature persistence. |
| Professional density | Equal | V2 hierarchy supports dense scanning without equal-weight widgets. |
| Chart productivity | Needs improvement | Advanced chart customization, drawing, and keyboard workflows are not the product's current strength. |
| Workspace customization | Needs improvement | Saved multi-panel, multi-monitor, and collaborative workspaces remain future work. |
| Dataset breadth | Needs improvement | Several evidence categories remain unavailable or partial by source contract. |
| Mobile continuity | Needs improvement | Responsive philosophy exists; complete V2 mobile journeys are not yet certified. |

## Patterns to Preserve

- Bloomberg: stable professional workspace and keyboard acceleration.
- TradingView: symbol/timeframe continuity and chart productivity.
- CoinGlass: rapid derivatives comparison and heatmaps.
- CryptoQuant/Glassnode: methodology and source transparency.
- SoSoValue: focused ETF/macro editorial hierarchy.
- Arkham: traceable object relationships and investigation continuity.

These patterns must be adapted through QuantTerminal's Evidence First and Human Decision Authority principles, never copied as feature accumulation.

## Decision

**PASS.** The unified journey is competitively differentiated and professionally coherent. Maturity gaps are implementation and breadth opportunities, not product-architecture defects.
