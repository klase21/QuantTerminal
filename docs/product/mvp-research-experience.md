# MVP Research Experience

## Product Contract

Research presents immutable governed Evidence as a user-facing argument, not an administrative inspector. The structured Projection remains authoritative.

The default hierarchy is:

1. Governed conclusion and one-sentence summary
2. Evidence strength, instrument, and Event Time
3. Verified facts
4. Rule-based interpretation
5. Supporting evidence
6. Counter evidence
7. Confidence and Coverage
8. Supplemental external context
9. Collapsed technical Evidence

Facts retain measured values and units. Interpretation is explicitly labeled as rule-based meaning rather than provider truth or a forecast. Supporting and counter evidence receive comparable visual weight. Evidence strength describes Coverage, alignment, agreement, freshness, and reproducibility; it is not future-price probability.

Projection IDs, packet IDs, checksums, source digests, rule versions, measurement versions, and dependency references remain available in the closed-by-default Technical Evidence disclosure. FRED DGS10, Alpha Vantage SPY, and Farside Bitcoin ETF Flow remain daily supplemental context and do not rewrite the crypto conclusion. Farside is publicly available through certified browser-backed scheduled retrieval; direct Node HTTP remains edge-rejected.
